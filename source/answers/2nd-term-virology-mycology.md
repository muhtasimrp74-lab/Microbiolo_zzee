**Answer**

**Bacteriophage**
- A **virus that infects bacteria**.
- **Structure:** tadpole-shaped (e.g. T-even phage), with a hexagonal **head** containing DNA, a hollow **tail** with sheath, a base plate and **tail fibres** for attachment.
- **Life cycles:**
  - **Lytic:** the phage multiplies, then the cell lyses and releases progeny.
  - **Lysogenic:** phage DNA integrates as a **prophage**, which replicates with the host chromosome.
- **Importance:**
  - **Lysogenic conversion** gives bacteria new properties: diphtheria toxin, botulinum toxins C & D, erythrogenic toxin, cholera toxin, Shiga toxin.
  - **Transduction** (gene transfer).
  - **Phage typing** of S. aureus and S. Typhi.
  - **Phage therapy** for drug-resistant infections.

**Viral interference**
- Infection of a cell by one virus **inhibits replication of a second virus** in the same cell.
- **Mechanisms:**
  - **Interferon** production.
  - Competition for or destruction of cell receptors.
  - **Defective interfering (DI) particles**.
- **Importance:**
  - OPV is not given with other enteric viral infections.
  - Interferon is used therapeutically (HBV, HCV).
  - Interference can cause vaccine failure.

**Pathogenesis of herpes labialis (HSV-1)**
1. **Primary infection:**
   - HSV-1 enters through oral mucosa or abraded skin and replicates in epithelial cells.
   - This causes **ballooning degeneration**, **multinucleated giant cells** and **Cowdry type A intranuclear inclusions**, which form intraepithelial **vesicles** (often gingivostomatitis in children).

## 454
**Answer**

**Role of reverse transcriptase in HBV replication.** HBV is a DNA virus that replicates through an **RNA intermediate**. The viral **polymerase** has **reverse transcriptase** activity.

1. After entry, the partially double-stranded **rcDNA** is repaired into **cccDNA** in the nucleus.
2. Host RNA polymerase II transcribes cccDNA into mRNAs and the **pregenomic RNA (pgRNA)**.
3. pgRNA and the viral polymerase are packaged into the **core particle** in the cytoplasm.
4. **Reverse transcriptase copies pgRNA into minus-strand DNA**, while its **RNase H** activity degrades the RNA template.
5. The polymerase then synthesises an incomplete plus strand, producing rcDNA. Core particles are enveloped or recycled to the nucleus.

**Significance**
- Reverse transcriptase is the target of **nucleos(t)ide analogues** (tenofovir, entecavir, lamivudine).
- Its lack of proofreading creates **mutants**, such as the YMDD lamivudine-resistance mutation and precore mutants.

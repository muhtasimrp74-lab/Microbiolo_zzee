**13. A 22-year-old women presents with dysuria and increased frequency of micturition. Most common causative organism is** — *SBA · Urinary Tract Infection*

- d) Enterococcus faecalis
- e) Escherichia coli
- **Answer: e**
- *Help link (book):* E. coli is responsible for about 80-90% of uncomplicated urinary tract infections. Although Staphylococcus saprophyticus occurs in young women, E. coli remains the most common cause.

**14. Which phase of bacterial growth shows an equal rate of cell division and cell death?** — *SBA · Bacterial Growth & Growth Curve*

- b) Lag phase
- c) Stationary phase
- d) Decline phase
- e) Exponential phase
- **Answer: c**
- *Help link (book):* During the stationary phase, nutrient depletion and accumulation of toxic products cause the rate of cell division to equal the rate of cell death.
